Professional

CredentAgent

Lead · Apache-2.0

The consent layer for AI agents. Before an agent does anything consequential (a payment, an age check, an access grant), it asks the wallet on your phone for proof: a verifiable credential held in secure hardware, sharing one fact and nothing more. Ships as an MCP App and speaks ISO mdoc, SD-JWT, OpenID4VP, and the W3C Digital Credentials API.
